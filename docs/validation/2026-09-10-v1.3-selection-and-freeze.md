## Explicit scoring decision

The original strict selector rejected Precise: as-reviewed and ambiguity-as-usable chose `cand-direct-google-twenty-fast`, while ambiguity-as-failure chose `cand-direct-google-balanced`. The preserved Windows PowerShell 5.1 diagnostic is `artifacts/v1.3-preparation/selection-diagnostic-ps51-20260910/rejection.json`. An earlier PowerShell 7 diagnostic failed at its JSON-array compatibility check and is not selection evidence.

After this rejection was explained, the owner explicitly selected **"Use conservative failure scoring (recommended)"** in this task on September 10. The accepted policy is `-AmbiguityPolicy ConservativeFailure`: uncertain labels remain ambiguous, receive zero usability credit, and stay in the denominator. Every role and the reported coverage use that pessimistic usability rate. Wrong-brand labels and their non-ambiguous denominator remain unchanged. All three replay outcomes are retained, including a false stability flag for Precise.

This is a disclosed decision made after observing sensitivity, not a claim that the original stability gate passed. The two leading precision candidates are close: conservative usability is 341/347 (98.2709%) for Direct Site + Google and 380/387 (98.1912%) for Direct Site + Google + Twenty Icons. The study does not establish a broad or statistically robust superiority claim.

The authorization record `artifacts/v1.3-preparation/conservative-policy-acceptance-20260910.json` binds the exact queue, prior spot-check and rejected strict replay. It supersedes only the earlier spot-check statement requiring ambiguity stability. Raw observations and labels are preserved.

Automatic approval review rejected reask-06's external crew execution because its snapshot could export repository context/local review data. It was not retried or bypassed. Codex completed the remaining adjudication locally with original images and official public references; those judgments are explicitly attributed to Codex. No permission request remains for that rejected route.

## Migration, rollout, recovery and communication

No database-format migration is introduced. Legacy Fast/Balanced/Thorough map to Fast/Balanced/Precise; Custom and unknown values map to Custom. Final-host checks must establish that Custom settings survive and repeated launches are idempotent.

Rollout: finish accepted selection, freeze source and version, pass source/CI gates, retain and test exact package bytes, capture real candidate screenshots, complete review, publish those verified assets, then deploy GitHub Pages and check downloaded hashes. Stable download links stay on the existing release until v1.3 assets are available.

Recovery: close KeePass, restore the backed-up plugin and affected configuration/database. Replacing the plugin alone does not undo downloaded icons. Final-host rollback must demonstrate this recovery on synthetic data.

Maintainer communication is the concrete validation packet with source identity, checksums and gate results. User-facing communication is the release notes, migration instructions, privacy limits and installation/recovery guidance published with the release. No external messages have been sent.
