PIXEL GROUND TRUTH. The pixel object is measured from the actual file and overrides any visual impression when they conflict: size is width x height in pixels; opaque_pct is the percent of pixels with alpha above 16 - zero means fully transparent; near_white_pct is the percent of opaque pixels that are near white; unique_colors counts distinct opaque RGBA values; top_colors lists the dominant color buckets as approximate RGB with percentages. Interpretation rules: opaque_pct of 0, or near_white_pct above 98 combined with unique_colors under 40, means the image is blank. unique_colors under about 120 with two dominant color buckets means a simple glyph on a plain background - typical of a generated letter mark. A rich palette above about 300 unique colors suggests a real brand or photographic icon. Use these numbers; do not argue with them.
