02

Availability-first selector

The first answer is not always the best answer.

Instead of treating every provider equally, KeeFetch lets candidates survive discovery and then compares them with explicit trust tiers.

Strong evidence moves forward.
Placeholders move back.

03

Privacy is a setting, not fine print

You decide how far KeeFetch may look.

Third-party favicon services improve availability, but they can receive domains from entries. KeeFetch discloses that tradeoff and lets you narrow the resolver surface.

More reach, or less exposure.

The default configuration keeps the availability-first resolver chain enabled. You can disable third-party providers, synthetic fallbacks, or individual resolvers from KeeFetch settings.

Tools → KeeFetch → Settings…
