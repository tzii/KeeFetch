Useful, not noisy

Six things KeeFetch does well

Direct-site parsing

Reads favicon links, apple-touch icons, and manifest declarations directly from the target site before stronger fallback lanes compete.

Availability-first ranking

Candidates are classified by trust tier so a placeholder-prone synthetic result cannot outrank a stronger real icon.

Bulk processing

Run KeeFetch on a single entry, a whole group, or your database maintenance flow without turning the UI into a waiting room.

Android URL mapping

Understands androidapp:// entries, maps packages to web domains, and can fall back through Play Store discovery.

SHA-256 dedup

Icons are content-addressed before storage, preventing the same image from being inserted over and over across entries.

Privacy controls

Disable third-party providers, synthetic fallbacks, or individual resolvers. KeeFetch has no telemetry or analytics.

◈ Privacy model

No telemetry. No analytics. No secret side-channel.

KeeFetch reads URL and title information locally. Direct Site contacts the target origin. When enabled fallback providers are needed, the target domain can be sent to those services to resolve an icon. Credentials, usernames, passwords, and full database contents are not sent.

Direct siteContacts the website you are already storing in the entry.
FallbacksCan receive the target domain only when those providers are enabled.
Your controlsDisable third-party or synthetic providers from KeeFetch settings.
