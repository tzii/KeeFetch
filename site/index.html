03 / Choose your approach

Your vault.
Your fetching style.

Four managed profiles in v1.3, with Balanced recommended for everyday use.

Fast

Large batch fetching with reduced latency

Direct Site → Yandex · 22 s total per-entry budget.

Enabled favicon resolvers may receive the entry’s domain. Synthetic fallbacks are disabled.

Balanced · Recommended

Default everyday use balancing coverage and speed

Direct Site → Google → Twenty Icons · 15 s total per-entry budget.

Enabled favicon resolvers may receive the entry’s domain. Synthetic fallbacks are disabled.

Privacy

Privacy-sensitive fetching without third-party providers

Direct Site · 22 s total per-entry budget.

No favicon resolvers or Android store lookup. Site-linked hosts and redirects remain possible. Synthetic fallbacks are disabled.

Precise

Favor icon precision over finding an icon for every entry

Direct Site → Google · 22 s total per-entry budget.

Enabled favicon resolvers may receive the entry’s domain. Synthetic fallbacks are disabled.

These controls preview v1.3 profiles; they do not change KeePass settings. The stable v1.2 download uses its earlier presets.

Know what leaves your device.

Your vault.
Your choices.

Fetching icons makes network requests. Third-party icon services may receive domains from your entries and can see your IP address.

You can disable individual providers or synthetic fallbacks in KeeFetch settings. Direct-site fetching may also contact icon hosts linked by a website.
