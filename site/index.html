Three steps.
A more familiar vault.

No new account. No new app to learn. Just a small addition to the KeePass you already use.

  1. Download KeeFetch.

    Get the plugin from the stable release. One small .plgx file.

  2. Give it a home.

    Copy it into the Plugins folder beside your KeePass executable.

  3. Fetch something familiar.

    Restart KeePass. Right-click an entry and choose KeeFetch – Download Favicons.
