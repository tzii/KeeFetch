Custom stays in your hands.

Choose enabled providers, their order, timeouts, synthetic fallbacks and Android store behavior. Selecting a managed profile applies that profile's policy; use Custom for manual choices. Settings edits take effect only on Save.

What each provider does

ProviderRole
Direct SiteFetches the target site, linked icons and manifests; can follow cross-host links and redirects.
Twenty Icons, DuckDuckGo, Google, YandexThird-party favicon resolvers. Enabled services may receive the target domain.
Favicone, Icon HorseAdditional third-party sources whose returned candidates may include generated fallbacks.

Selection ranks site-canonical candidates, strong resolved candidates, then synthetic fallbacks. Enabling a provider does not guarantee it will produce a usable icon.

Privacy and synthetic icons

Privacy disables favicon resolvers and Android store lookup. Direct Site still contacts the target and may fetch linked assets on another host. A synthetic fallback is a generated representation such as a letter mark, used when no stronger real icon survives selection.
