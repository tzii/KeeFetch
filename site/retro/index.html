01 / Built for your vault

Good icons. Less busywork.

The useful details, handled.
You have enough entries to look after.

01

Go straight to the source.

Read website icon declarations, touch icons and manifests before ranking the available candidates.

02

Better than a blurry shortcut.

Compare the available icon candidates, including the larger icons sites provide for mobile devices.

03

A whole group. One fetch.

Process entries and subgroups together. Concurrent requests and caching cut out repetitive work.

04

Android entries, included.

Map androidapp:// package names to web domains, with built-in mappings and a Play Store fallback.

05

Keep the icon. Skip the copy.

SHA-256 checks detect duplicate icons, so entries can reuse what is already in your database.

06

You pick the providers.

Choose a fetching preset, manage individual providers, and control synthetic fallback icons.

03 / Choose your approach

A quick tidy.
Or a proper deep clean.

Four presets for different kinds of fetching.

Fast

Fewer requests. Less waiting.

For a quick pass through your vault. Prioritize speed rather than checking every available source.

Balanced

A little speed. A little thoroughness.

The default v1.2 preset uses Direct Site, Google and Favicone to balance speed and icon coverage.

Thorough

Give the elusive ones another look.

For missing or difficult icons. Check the enabled provider lanes and compare more candidates.

Custom

Your providers. Your order.

Manage provider selection, ordering and fallback choices yourself in KeeFetch settings.

v1.2 preset guide. These controls only change this preview, not your plugin settings.

Know what leaves your device.

Your vault.
Your choices.

Fetching icons makes network requests. Third-party icon services may receive domains from your entries and can see your IP address.

You can disable individual providers or synthetic fallbacks in KeeFetch settings. Direct-site fetching may also contact icon hosts linked by a website.
